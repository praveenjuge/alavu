Common questions

Does Alavu work offline? Yes. Entries are saved on your iPhone and sync when you're back online.

Can I keep work money separate? Yes. Add a space for it from the title menu at the top of the app.

How do I delete my data? Settings → Delete Account removes everything permanently.
