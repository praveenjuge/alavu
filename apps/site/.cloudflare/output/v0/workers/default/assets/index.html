Alavu

Know where your money goes.

Alavu is a simple expense and income tracker for iPhone. Keep separate spaces for personal money, work, or a group fund, set monthly budgets, and log entries in seconds, even offline.

What you can do

  • Track expenses, income, and transfers between spaces.
  • Set monthly budgets that can follow your payday.
  • Add repeating entries like rent or salary once.
  • Attach receipt photos.
  • Log an expense from Siri, Shortcuts, widgets, or Control Center.

Alavu doesn't connect to your bank. You decide what to record.
