For iPhone and the web

Know where your money goes.

Alavu is a calm, simple expense tracker. Keep personal, work and family money in separate spaces, set budgets that follow your payday, and log an expense in seconds.

Open Alavu on the web iPhone app coming soon to the App Store

Everything you need, nothing you don’t.

  • Spaces for every part of life

    Keep personal money, work and a family fund apart, each with its own currency, categories and budgets.

  • Budgets that follow payday

    Start your month on the day you get paid and see what’s left at a glance, overall or by category.

  • Quick to add

    Log an entry in a few taps, or without opening the app: ask Siri, tap a widget, or use Control Center.

  • Repeating entries

    Add rent, salary and subscriptions once. Alavu records them on schedule, even when the app is closed.

  • Receipts kept with entries

    Attach a photo of the bill to the entry it belongs to, so it’s there when you need it.

  • iPhone and web, in sync

    Works offline on iPhone and stays in step with the web app the moment you’re back online.

Private by design.

Alavu never connects to your bank, shows no ads and never sells your data. You decide what to record, and you can delete your account and everything in it whenever you like. Read the privacy policy.
